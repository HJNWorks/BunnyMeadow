# Wildlife — Silver Bat

Status: live (Chapter 2 Quiet Wells). Class: Wildlife. Archetype: diver.
Hub: [../README.md](../README.md). Invented lunar fauna. Bats are 福 (fortune) in
Chinese symbolism ([LORE.md](../../LORE.md#bats-as-fortune-蝠-福)), so this is not an
ill omen. Not a moon servant.

## Behaviour

Hangs and drifts under the cave roof. When Mei is within 420 px sideways it swoops
at her for 1.0 s (speed 320), then flaps back to its roost. Same diver kit as the
owl, with a longer swoop so it can reach the lips from the roof.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: it drifts over the lip you are about to use. Counter: keep moving across
the lip, dash through the swoop, or wait for it to go back up.

## Silhouette rule

Grey-silver wings with pale edges, small ears, pale eyes. Reads as a cave bat, not an
owl, not a frost wisp.
