## Behaviour

A slow drifting cloud that denies an area. It has no single body to defeat; the player
routes around it or dashes through, since a dash passes safely.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: a visible buzzing haze marking the denied zone. Counter: dash straight
through, or wait for the cloud to drift off the path.

## Silhouette rule

A loose particle cloud, not a sprite. Reads as area, not enemy.
