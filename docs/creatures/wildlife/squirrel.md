## Behaviour

Throws acorns on a parabola like the crow, but from tree cover and at a faster
cadence with a shorter arc. Rewards reading two lobbers at once in the orchard.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: tail flick and a hop before each throw. Counter: close the distance; its
arc undershoots at short range.

## Silhouette rule

Bushy upright tail, small body. The tail is the read.
