# Wildlife — Owl

Status: live (Story). Class: Wildlife. Archetype: diver. Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).

Guard: owls are ill omens in Chinese tradition and must never serve the Moon. They are
ordinary night enemies only, never Moon servants. See
[../../LORE.md](../../LORE.md#cranes-as-immortal-messengers).

## Behaviour

Circles overhead, then dives along a shown line. The night counterpart to the crow:
vertical threat instead of a lobbed arc.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: it stops circling and a dive line draws before the plunge. Counter: step
off the line during the wind-up; the dive commits once started.

## Silhouette rule

Round head, broad wings, front-facing eyes. The drawn dive line is the true tell.
