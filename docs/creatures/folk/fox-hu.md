# Folk — Fox Hu

Status: live. Class: Folk (World 1 boss, recurring trickster). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [adapted](../../LORE.md#fox-spirits--huli-jing-狐狸精). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The recurring trickster of the meadow. He steals the carrot basket the kits need and
blocks paths, but he is never truly dangerous, matching the family tone. Named Hu, not
Wu, to avoid clashing with Wu Gang.

## Boss encounter

World 1 finale, Cart Chase: a comic carrot-cart race. Win by reaching the burrow
basket first or tipping the cart. Teaches sustained dashing under pressure. Achievement
`FOX_FOILED`. The cart is seated on the ground by `constrainCreatureToWorld` after it
joins the enemy group (group add would otherwise turn gravity back on with no floor
collider).

## Relation to wildlife

The plain [fox](../wildlife/fox.md) chaser is the roster version. Fox Hu is the scripted
named version and does not spawn from biome rosters.

## Silhouette rule

Same fox read, plus his carrot cart, which is his signature prop.
