## Behaviour

Walks a crater lip sideways and turns at the edge (same patrol kit as the hedgehog,
speed 42). Every 2.4–3.2 s it digs into the dust for 1.3 s: it fades, stops, throws a
little dust, and cannot hurt Mei or be stomped. Then it pops back up.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: a dust puff and a fade when it digs, another puff when it comes up.
Counter: stomp it while it is up, cross while it is dug in, or hop over it.

## Silhouette rule

A grey shell with a pale crater ring on top, stalk eyes, small claws. Reads as moon
rock, not a red river crab.
