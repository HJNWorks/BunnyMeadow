## Behaviour

Swims just under the still silver of the nearest water hazard, turning at its edges
(speed 55). Every one to two seconds it leaps: 420 px/s up for 0.6 s, about 250 px
above the surface, then drops back. The river carp only breaks the surface. This one
is practising for the Dragon Gate.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: a pale shape sliding under the skin you are crossing. Counter: keep moving,
time the crossing after a leap, or stomp it on the way down.

## Silhouette rule

Pale silver body with scale arcs, a dark eye, a small barbel. Reads as a carp, not a
star wisp and not Still Silver.
