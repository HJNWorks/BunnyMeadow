# Wildlife — Carp

Status: live (Story Floating Logs). Class: Wildlife. Archetype: water_patrol. Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
No folklore role; plain wildlife. First water-bound enemy. Hovers in the water band
and breaches.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Home biomes | riverbank |
| Active hour | dusk |
| Min tier | 3 |
| Hearts | 1 |
| Safe from above | yes (in water) |

## Behaviour

Idea: patrols just under the water surface and breaches to slap a passing log, nudging
the player off. It makes the water itself feel alive rather than only a fall hazard.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: a shadow and ripple track under the surface before the breach. Counter: be
airborne or on a different log when it breaches.

## Silhouette rule

Broad-backed fish; only the shadow and ripple are visible until it breaches.
