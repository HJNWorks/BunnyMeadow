# Kit — Yue

Status: live. Class: Kits (the missing kit). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [invented](../../LORE.md). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The youngest kit, called "Moon". She glows faintly. Chasing the fallen osmanthus
blossom, she is mistaken by the Crane Envoy for the blossom itself and carried toward
the moon. She is the reason for the climb.

## Guard

No predator kidnapping and no death. Yue goes missing by misunderstanding only and is
safe the whole time, playing with the Jade Rabbit in the palace. The finale reveals
this.

## Presence in play

Not playable. She appears in the opening beat and the finale, not on the field.
