## Behaviour

A jade haze that denies a cave mouth. Same swarm kit as frost wisp and dust mite.
Route around it, or dash through. A dash passes safely.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: pale jade cloud in a false mouth. Counter: dash through, or wait for
the cloud to drift.

## Silhouette rule

Loose jade grit, not a person. Reads as grotto stone, not lunar dust.

## Bunny Jump

Skip. West of Silver is Story, not Bunny Jump.
