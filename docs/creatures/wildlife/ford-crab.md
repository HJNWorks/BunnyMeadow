## Behaviour

Walks the wide ford stones sideways. Never enters the water: Weak Water bears no
crab either. Unlike the crater crab it does not dig in. Texture `story_fordcrab`,
kit entry `ford_crab` in `enemyKit.ts`.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: the sideways shuffle. Counter: hop over it or stomp from above.

## Silhouette rule

River-grey crab with a mossy shell. Reads as ford wildlife, not a lunar crater.
