## Behaviour

Perches and lobs a projectile on a parabola with a visible wind-up. The most
widespread ranged threat, tuning the player to watch the sky as well as the ground.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: wing raise and a brief caw before the throw. Counter: move during the
wind-up; the arc is fixed once thrown.

## Silhouette rule

Angular perched bird, beak forward. The raised wing reads as the danger frame.
