## Behaviour

A pale dust cloud that denies a rim or courtyard gap. Same swarm kit as frost wisp.
Route around it, or dash through. A dash passes safely.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: ash-coloured haze. Counter: dash through, or wait for the cloud to drift.

## Silhouette rule

Loose oval dust, not a person. Reads as lunar grit, not Cloud Stair frost.
