## Behaviour

Perches on a copper pin and lobs a pebble on a parabola. Same kit as ice spit and
pestle sentry. It guards a ledge. Earth-weight hops. Standing still on a pin is a
bad idea.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: the sentry coils, then the pebble leaves. Counter: hop the lob.

## Silhouette rule

Small copper pin, mineral brown. Reads as ridge metal, not a palace lady, not a
spaceship gun.
