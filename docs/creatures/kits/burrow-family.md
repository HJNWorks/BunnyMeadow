# Kits — The Burrow Family

Status: live (background). Class: Kits. Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [invented](../../LORE.md). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The rabbits of the home burrow: Mei and Yue's family and the other kits. They frame the
start and the end of the night. The burrow is the hub of every mode and the safe zone
that chasers leave alone.

## Presence in play

Background and framing only. The burrow entrance is the Meadow landmark and the pantry
lives here ([../../universe/environments/burrow-tunnels.md](../../universe/environments/burrow-tunnels.md)).
The epilogue is the family reunion under the full moon.

## Guard

Family tone throughout. No death, no real peril to the family.
