# Folk — Wu Gang

Status: live. Class: Folk (finale NPC). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [canon](../../LORE.md#wu-gang-吳剛). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The woodcutter banished to the Moon, forever chopping an osmanthus (cassia) tree that
heals itself after every blow. In the finale he gives one hint, then keeps chopping.
His tree is the source of the glowing blossom that starts the whole story when one falls
to earth.

## Link to the world

The osmanthus blossom fall is the plot's inciting event and the source of the osmanthus
blossom item ([../../items/catalog.md](../../items/catalog.md)). His tree is canon; the
fall event is invented ([../../LORE.md](../../LORE.md)).

## Presence in play

Non-hostile palace NPC on the Moon rung under his tree. Does not spawn from rosters.
