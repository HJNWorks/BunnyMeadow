## Behaviour

A night reach enemy. Sits on a ledge and swipes a long paw in one direction on a
crouch telegraph, guarding festival rooftops and mountain ledges. Live on Paper Lights.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: haunches raise and tail lashes before the swipe. Counter: cross after the
swipe; glide over it where a lantern glide zone allows.

## Silhouette rule

Compact crouch, ears and lashing tail. The raised haunches read as the wind-up.
