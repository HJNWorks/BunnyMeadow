# Folk — Chang'e

Status: live. Class: Folk (mentor, finale). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [canon](../../LORE.md#change-嫦娥). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The Moon. She speaks to Mei through water reflections at every Moon Pool checkpoint,
one gentle line at a time, and lights a pool each night to guide the climb. In the
finale at Guanghan Palace she asks Mei to stay, because the palace is lonely, which is
canon. She then lets the kits go.

## Presence in play

Not an enemy and not a walking NPC on the field. Her channel is the Moon Pool: touching
a pool saves progress and may play one Chang'e line (see [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md)).
Home rung: the Moon ([../../universe/environments/moon.md](../../universe/environments/moon.md)).

## Guard

Reflections are her only channel. Keep exchanges to one line. Do not contradict her
loneliness and authority.
