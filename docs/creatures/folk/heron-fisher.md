# Folk — Heron Fisher

Status: live. Class: Folk (World 2 boss). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [invented](../../LORE.md) (herons are ordinary wildlife, not folklore deities).
Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

An invented river antagonist who fishes the rafts of the Riverbank. Sporty rather than
menacing: a test of the log ride and dash learned on the valley floor.

## Boss encounter

World 2 finale, Raft Gauntlet: survive the raft run and land a dash on the heron three
times. Teaches committing a dash on a moving platform.

## Relation to wildlife

The plain [heron](../wildlife/heron.md) reach enemy is the roster version. The Heron
Fisher is the scripted named version with phases.

## Silhouette rule

Tall heron read, plus a fishing pose and the rafts, which frame the fight.
