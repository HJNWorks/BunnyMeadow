## Behaviour

A weak night flyer that drifts toward lanterns, sparklers, and palace screens. Short
stoop along a shown line, slower than owl or magpie. Contact costs a heart. Dash
through is safe.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: wings flare toward the nearest light, then a shallow dive. Counter: wait
the pass, or dash through.

## Silhouette rule

Pale moth, paper-thin wings, drawn to warm light. Not a crane. Not an owl serving the
Moon.

## Bunny Jump

Skip until a drift role needs a second moth. Owl already covers stoop.
