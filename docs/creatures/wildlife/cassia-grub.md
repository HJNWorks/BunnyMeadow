## Behaviour

A pale grub that rode Wu Gang's bark chips from the Cassia Wound. It crawls a bowl
rim or a roller trough and turns at the edge. Slow (30). Same patrol kit as the
hedgehog.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: it only ever crawls toward the edge it faces. Counter: stomp it from
above, or hop over it. A roller passing over it does nothing. Rollers do not
touch wildlife.

## Silhouette rule

Cream segments, a brown head, gold bark flecks. Reads as a grub from the cassia
tree, not a moon toad and not a caterpillar from the Earth rungs.
