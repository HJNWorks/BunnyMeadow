# Folk — Jade Rabbit (Yutu)

Status: live. Class: Folk (palace NPC). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [canon](../../LORE.md#jade-rabbit--yutu-玉兔). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

Chang'e's companion on the Moon, who pounds the elixir with mortar and pestle. In the
finale he is the comic NPC who has been looking after Yue in the palace, which is how
the player learns the missing kit was always safe.

## Guard

Chinese tradition only. Do not import the Japanese mochi-pounding rabbit or mochi.
Mooncakes are correct; mochi is not ([../../LORE.md](../../LORE.md#jade-rabbit--yutu-玉兔)).

## Presence in play

Non-hostile palace NPC on the Moon rung
([../../universe/environments/moon.md](../../universe/environments/moon.md)). Does not
spawn from rosters.
