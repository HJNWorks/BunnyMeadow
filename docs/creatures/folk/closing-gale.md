# Folk — Closing Gale

Status: live. Class: Folk event (World 4-3 chase). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [invented](../../LORE.md). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

Vast Cold leaking down the Cloud Stair as a storm wall. It is Han's outer habit of
turning guests back, not a named immortal, and not Chang'e throwing weather. Same
family-tone fail as Fox Hu's cart: retry the station.

## Encounter

World 4 finale, Closing Gale. A tall wind wall closes from the left. Reach the
palace gate first. A fall into the cloud, or the wall catching Mei, fails the
station. There is no Moon Pool restore. No dash-hit HP bar.

## Guard

Do not use Fengbo, Yu Shi, Xiwangmu, or Qixi magpies as this event. The gale has
no face. Magpies on the stair are ordinary wildlife
([../wildlife/magpie.md](../wildlife/magpie.md)).

## Silhouette rule

A wide pale storm sheet, not a cart and not a person. It fills the height of the
stair so Mei cannot hop over it.
