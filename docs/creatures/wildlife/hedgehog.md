# Wildlife — Hedgehog

Status: live. Class: Wildlife. Archetype: patrol. Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).

## Behaviour

Walks a fixed path with contact damage. Unlike most patrols it is not safe from above
because of its spines, so it teaches the player to go around rather than bounce.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: steady predictable pace, no surprise. Counter: time a gap in its patrol or
dash across.

## Silhouette rule

Round, low, spined dome. Slow read, clearly not a threat to rush.

removes it (Bunny Jump stomp, even with spines). Same stamp as Story. Design:
