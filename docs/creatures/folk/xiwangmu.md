## Role

Queen Mother of the West. Holds peaches of immortality. Hou Yi obtained elixir
from her in common tellings. Chang'e drank that source and rose to the moon.

In West of Silver she offers a peach. Mei refuses. She accepts the refusal. Mei
stays a kit who waves at the moon. Yue is already home. Rescue is closed.

## Presence in play

Non-hostile court NPC at West Pool. Does not spawn from rosters. Map cards do
not print Kunlun, Yaochi, portal, or 西王母.

## Guard

Never an HP bar. Same off-limits list as Chang'e, Yue, Yutu, Wu Gang, moon toad,
Crane Envoy, and Fox Hu. No second Han. Optional invented steward (if added
later) tests then bows. That steward is not her.

## Silhouette rule

Court presence, not a giant goddess portrait. Family Mid-Autumn tone.
