## Role

Queen Mother of the West. Holds peaches of immortality. Hou Yi obtained elixir
from her in common tellings. Chang'e drank that source and rose to the moon.

In West of Silver she offers a peach after the pool seal surfaces. Mei refuses.
The seal does not take the peach either. She accepts the refusal. Mei stays a kit
who waves at the moon. Yue is already home. Rescue is closed.

## Presence in play

Non-hostile court presence at West Pool. The offer is a ticker on the far terrace,
not a sprite. Does not spawn from rosters. Map cards do not print Kunlun, Yaochi,
portal, or 西王母.

## Guard

Never an HP bar. Same off-limits list as Chang'e, Yue, Yutu, Wu Gang, moon toad,
Crane Envoy, and Fox Hu. The pool's settling spirit is the
[old face](old-face.md), not a steward of hers, and not a second Han.

## Silhouette rule

Court presence, not a giant goddess portrait. Family Mid-Autumn tone.
