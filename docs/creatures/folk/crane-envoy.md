# Folk — Crane Envoy

Status: live. Class: Folk (World 3 boss, then ally). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [adapted](../../LORE.md#cranes-as-immortal-messengers). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The Moon's messenger, sent to bring the fallen osmanthus blossom back. It mistakes the
glowing kit Yue for the blossom and carries Yue toward the moon. It is an antagonist by
misunderstanding, not malice, and becomes the ally who carries Mei to the Cloud Stair
and later home from Guanghan.

## Boss encounter

World 3 finale, Crane Summit: dodge three dives, then accept the ride when it bows.
Archetype behaviour is diver, then reach, then a scripted bow-and-carry. Achievement
`CRANE_FRIEND`. Soft resolution, not a defeat.

## Guard

Cranes are the Moon's messenger here. Owls must never take this role
([../wildlife/owl.md](../wildlife/owl.md)).

## Silhouette rule

Long neck and broad white wings. The drawn dive line telegraphs the attack; the bow
pose signals the turn to ally.
