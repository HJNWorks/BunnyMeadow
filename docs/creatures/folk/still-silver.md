## Role

The body of the One Moon Pool when a stranger nears. Not a Western water elemental.
Not a Dragon King or Hebo. A quiet silver mass that keeps the first true pool until
Mei proves soft paws. Ordinary Moon Pools stay Chang'e's reflection channel. This pool
does not speak a ticker line.

## Encounter

Station `ch2_silver_3_one_pool`. Module: `src/modes/story/shared/stillBoss.ts`.

Heart budget: **1 + 4 + 8 = 13** (HUD glyphs like Han, not a text counter).

| Size | Hearts | Motion | Abilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full | 1 | Slow grounded patrol on the shore belt | Charged stream. Dual tsunami |
| Quarter | 1 each (4) | Faster grounded patrol | Stream only |
| Eighth | 1 each (8) | Hectic small hops | None |

Mooncake buff opens a short damaging-dash window (~2.8s). One dash can hit several
bodies. Warmth is not cleared on the first hit. Contact without the buff hurts Mei.

Dash on Full → four Quarters. Dash on a Quarter → two Eighths. Dash on an Eighth →
one inert **aspect of water** mote on the rim.

When eight aspects exist they gather over the One Moon Pool into a single portal.
Mei contacts that portal to clear the station and open **West of Silver (Chapter 3)**.
No burrow exit on this station.

## Silhouette rule

Low, wide, still water with a soft crest. Full stamp ~3× prior display. Quarter and
Eighth are half-steps down. Drip, trail, and splash VFX stay family-soft.
