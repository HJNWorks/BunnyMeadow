## Behaviour

Holds a line and charges when the player crosses it, then is stunned on a wall hit.
It converts a corridor into a timing puzzle: bait the charge, then pass. Stamp is
live. No Chapter 1 chunk places this id yet. I8 placement.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: head lowers and a hoof scrapes before the charge. Counter: bait it into a
wall; cross during the stun window.

## Silhouette rule

Squared body, lowered horns. The head-down scrape is the wind-up frame.

## Bunny Jump

Jump role: ledge_charge. Status: planned ([I5](../../iterations/i5-bunny-jump.md)).
Charges across a wide pad. Side contact costs a heart. Landing from above removes it
or uses the Story stun if the charge hits a pad edge. Same stamp. Design:
[../../modes/tasks/bunny-jump.md](../../modes/tasks/bunny-jump.md).
