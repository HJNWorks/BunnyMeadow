## Behaviour

A short hop patrol on pale steps and hanging bridges. Inside a sense radius it can
chase. Hop over, or dash through. Do not name this stamp Yutu or a kit.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: ears flatten and a freeze-breath puff before a hop. Counter: jump the
body or dash through the line. Side contact costs a heart.

## Silhouette rule

White mountain hare, bristled winter coat, no jade pestle, no armor, no glow like Yue.
Reads as weather-sick wildlife, not palace folk.

## Bunny Jump

Skip. Cloud Stair is Story. Do not import this id onto Earth rungs.

## Guard

Giant pandas and yeti stay out. White hares on the Stair are this invented job only.
