# Folk — Tu'er Ye (Lord Rabbit) and the Tiger

Status: live. Class: Folk (World 3 ally). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [canon](../../LORE.md#tuer-ye--lord-rabbit-兔兒爺). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The armored Mid-Autumn folk rabbit of Lantern Village. He lends his tiger for the
ascent up Osmanthus Peak. His presence at the festival is itself canon.

## The tiger

The tiger is his mount, not a separate enemy. In Tiger Road (3-2) the player rides it
in a scripted sequence up the mountain. The ride is a movement verb taught on the
Lantern rung ([../abilities.md](../abilities.md)).

## Relation to wildlife

Non-hostile. Neither Tu'er Ye nor the tiger spawns from biome rosters.

## Silhouette rule

Armored upright rabbit, often astride the striped tiger. The pairing is the read.
