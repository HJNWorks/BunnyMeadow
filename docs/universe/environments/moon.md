## Feel

Low gravity, quiet platforming, the Guanghan Palace and Wu Gang's tree. This is a
calm rung, the reward after the climb. No boss. Floats replace running; falls read as
gentle drifts, not deaths.

## Roster

Wildlife: none. This rung is peopled by folk, not enemies.

Folk (see [../../creatures/folk/](../../creatures/folk/)):

## Endless status

Moon is Story-only by default. In Endless it is the terminal reward rung, reachable
only when a run holds the moon letter key ([../../items/catalog.md](../../items/catalog.md))
and crosses the Cloud Sea. This is an idea, not scheduled.

## Render notes

Eternal-night sky, star drift, low-gravity float tuning on the player controller.
Night overlay always on. Weather preset: slow blossom fall and star drift.
