Status: planned (I8). Class: Wildlife. Archetype: blocker. Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).

Chapter 1 Path (orchard or bamboo), not Chapter 3 Later.

## Behaviour

Idea: a longer, faster charge than the goat with more recovery, and possibly two
hearts so it survives one wall hit. It would give the orchard a heavy that anchors a
lane the player must clear before a lobber above.

## Telegraph and counter

Telegraph: snort and a longer paw-back than the goat. Counter: same bait-into-wall,
but two charges may be needed.

## Silhouette rule

Bulky, low, tusked. Reads heavier and slower to stop than the goat.
