# Kit — Mei

Status: live. Class: Kits (player avatar). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [invented](../../LORE.md). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The eldest kit of the burrow and the player character. Fast and brave. She is the one
who talks to the Moon through water and runs the whole climb to bring her sibling home.

## Abilities

Mei carries every player verb: run, jump, dash, wall bounce, log ride, tiger ride,
glide, low-gravity float. Where each is taught is on [../abilities.md](../abilities.md).

## Cosmetics

Mei is the sprite the Customize screen dresses (`drawBunny`). Pantry cosmetics never
change stats (a GDD scope guard). Beating Han also unlocks a moon helmet, the same
rule as achievement dashes. It is cosmetic only.

## Guard

Must not contradict Chang'e's loneliness and authority.
