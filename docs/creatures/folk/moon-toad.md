# Folk — Moon Toad (Chanchu)

Status: live (optional background). Class: Folk (background NPC). Hub: [../README.md](../README.md).
LORE: [canon, optional](../../LORE.md#moon-toad--chanchu-蟾蜍). Story: [../../STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Role

The oldest moon companion in Chinese visual tradition, a three-legged toad on the lunar
disc, later largely replaced in popular telling by the Jade Rabbit. Here it is an
optional background presence in Guanghan Palace and never the antagonist.

## Guard

Background only. It must not become an enemy or a boss.

## Presence in play

Optional decoration on the Moon rung
([../../universe/environments/moon.md](../../universe/environments/moon.md)). Does not
spawn from rosters.
